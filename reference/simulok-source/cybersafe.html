Interactive training for students & future professionals
Whether you're working on case competitions, internship projects, or entering the corporate world — you will handle confidential data. This training prepares you to recognise phishing, protect sensitive data from AI leaks, and use AI responsibly.
Module 1

Email Phishing

Recognise fraudulent emails, malicious links, and social engineering tactics. From fake recruiter emails to scholarship scams — learn the red flags and the response protocol.

Module 2

AI & Confidential Data

Understand why confidential data must never leave the organisation's boundary through public AI tools. Whether it's a case competition dataset, internship project, or client data — learn data classification and safe AI practices.

Module 3

AI Guardrails

AI is your most powerful productivity tool — and most dangerous if used blindly. Learn about hallucinations, prompt injection, bias, and why human verification is non-negotiable.
