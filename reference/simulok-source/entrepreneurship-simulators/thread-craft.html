📚 The Challenge

Over 15 months, navigate trend forecasting, supply chain decisions, sustainability trade-offs, competitor warfare, and two funding rounds. Build ThreadCraft from a small online brand to a ₹100 Cr+ fashion empire — or learn valuable lessons trying.

You start with ₹25,00,000 in cash. Every month brings new decisions: what to design, where to manufacture, how to market, and when to raise funding.

📊 Metrics You'll Track

Cash — Your bank balance. Run out and it's game over.

Trend Score — How well you predict what customers want (0-100).

Brand Value — How strong your brand is in customers' minds (0-100).

Sustainability Rating — Your environmental & ethical score (0-100).

Dead Stock % — Unsold inventory that eats your margins.

Valuation — What investors think your company is worth.
