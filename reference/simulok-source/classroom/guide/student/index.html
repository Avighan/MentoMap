1. Sign in

  1. Open https://simulok.in/platform/.
  2. Stay on the Participant tab.
  3. Enter:
    • Student ID — e.g. MBA01 (from your handout)
    • Your name — so it appears on the class scoreboard
    • Password — from the same handout
  4. Click Sign in.
Wait until your instructor says the session is open. If no session is open, you may not get the assigned simulation.

2. Play the simulation

  • You are taken into the simulation your instructor assigned (for example Mumbai, Zara, or Zenith).
  • Read each round carefully. Your choices are saved as you go.
  • If the title is Zenith Appliances, a separate page may open — stay on that tab until you finish.

3. Finish and your score

  1. Complete every round until you reach the results screen.
  2. Your final score is sent to the instructor automatically.
  3. Your instructor can see your name and how you did.

4. Rules to remember

  • One completion per session unless your instructor allows another attempt.
  • Do not refresh to try again after you finish — you will usually be blocked.
  • If you lose connection mid-run, ask the instructor before reloading.
  • Use the name you want on the class scoreboard.

5. If something goes wrong

“Unknown ID” or wrong password

Check your Student ID and password with the instructor. IDs look like MBA01, not an email.

“You already completed this session”

You finished once already. Ask the instructor if a retake is allowed.

Wrong simulation

The instructor may not have opened a session yet. Wait for their go-ahead.

Still stuck?

Ask your instructor. Do not use Demo User during a graded sitting.
